# Reflection: clue-developers_clue

CLUE is a research library that goes with a paper about reducing differential equation models. Its activity is irregular, with bursts like 99 commits in 2020-04 and 116 in 2023-03 and quiet months in between. There are 4 gaps of 3+ months and the longest is 2020-05 to 2020-08 (4 months).

Before the gap, the commits were experiment results and README edits that added the paper title and authors, and the paper went up on arXiv that same week. There are no issues or PRs from that time, so my guess is the authors stopped while the paper was being reviewed.

Work started again in September 2020 with a new .ode parser and other features, probably for the journal version. Gleb Pogudin made most of those commits and Joshua Jacob joined as a new contributor. The last GitHub commit is 2024-09-12, so it is Inactive now.
